## 1. Executive Summary & Tafsir Thematic Grouping

Surah An-Nahl is traditionally titled *Surat an-Ni'am* (The Chapter of Divine Favors/Bounties). It systematically articulates the signs of Allah throughout the cosmos, the biological and geological order of creation, the divine inspiration given to the bee, social justice, and the monotheistic legacy of Prophet Ibrahim (AS).

In adherence to strict Islamic sensitivities:
- **No anthropomorphic depiction of Allah.**
- **No physical depiction, silhouette, or actor representing any Prophet or Angel.**
- **Cinematic focus:** Majesty of untouched landscapes, cosmic spheres, natural geological formations, flowing streams, botanical flora, and celestial dawns.
