## 1. Executive Summary & Tafsir Thematic Grouping

Surah Al-Isra opens with the transcendent Night Journey of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) from Al-Masjid al-Haram to Al-Masjid al-Aqsa, followed by profound lessons from Bani Isra'il history, individual moral accountability (deeds fastened to each person's neck), supreme kindness to parents, comprehensive societal prohibitions, the praise of Allah by the seven heavens and earth, the nature of the human spirit (Ar-Ruh), the challenge of divine miracles, and steadfast devotion in prayer and praise.

In adherence to strict Islamic sensitivities:
- **No anthropomorphic depiction of Allah.**
- **No physical depiction, silhouette, or actor representing any Prophet or Angel.**
- **Cinematic focus:** Majesty of untouched landscapes, cosmic spheres, natural geological formations, flowing streams, botanical flora, and celestial dawns.
